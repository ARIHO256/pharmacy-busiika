# Professional Workflow Sign-off

Each workflow must be accepted by a qualified representative using realistic seeded or anonymized cases. Acceptance is release-specific and expires when the workflow, policy, integration, or legal requirement materially changes.

## Required groups

- Doctor or medical director.
- Nurse and midwife.
- Laboratory professional.
- Pharmacist.
- Accountant or finance manager.
- Medical records officer.
- Platform/operations administrator.

## Minimum scenario evidence

The reviewer must complete the role’s critical journey from login to final persisted outcome, verify permissions and negative cases, check printed/downloaded artifacts, and record limitations. The evidence should include case identifiers, timestamps, screenshots or test exports, and reviewer signature.

## Prohibited practice

Do not accept a gate based only on page presence, route counts, mock screenshots, or a developer statement. Clinical and financial acceptance requires the resulting database state and downstream effects to be verified.
