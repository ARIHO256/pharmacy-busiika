# Legal, Privacy, and Clinical-Safety Acceptance

This document defines the evidence expected before real patient data is processed. It is not legal advice and does not claim regulatory certification.

## Privacy and legal

- Data-controller/processor responsibilities and contracts.
- Lawful bases, consent, guardian/dependent authority, and withdrawal.
- Privacy notice, retention, disclosure, and patient-rights procedures.
- Cross-border transfer and subprocessor review.
- Incident and breach notification procedure.
- Protected-record and sensitive-note rules.
- Regulatory and professional licensing review for the deployment country.

## Clinical safety

- Named clinical-safety officer.
- Hazard log and risk controls.
- Validation of medication, laboratory, imaging, triage, maternity, theatre, emergency, and discharge workflows.
- Downtime and degraded-mode procedures.
- Critical-result escalation and acknowledgment.
- Signed-record immutability and amendment process.
- Human-factors review for high-risk interfaces.
- Training and competency requirements.
