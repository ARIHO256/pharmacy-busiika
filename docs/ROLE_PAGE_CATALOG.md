# Role Page and Workflow Catalogue

## Patient / caregiver

Pages:

- Home: appointments, unpaid invoices, prescriptions, results and alerts.
- Find care: verified providers, specialty, facility and fee information.
- Appointments: the patient's and authorized dependents' bookings.
- Consultations: secure messages and video session entry.
- Health record: timeline, allergies, conditions, medications, observations, care plans and referrals.
- Prescriptions: issued and dispensed medication.
- Laboratory: orders, specimen progress and released results.
- Pharmacy: medicine browsing, cart, prescription routing and orders.
- Hospitals: verified facilities, services and bookings.
- Hospital care: admissions, imaging and discharge records.
- Insurance: coverage, eligibility and claims.
- Payments and invoices: balances, payment initiation, PDF invoice and receipt.
- Family and dependents: separate patient profiles and caregiver authority.
- Subscription and support.
- Profile, consent, notification preferences and security centre.

## Doctor / clinical officer / dentist / medical director

Pages:

- Clinical home: today's schedule, alerts and performance.
- Availability: work periods, breaks and holidays.
- Appointments: accept, reject, complete, cancel and mark no-show.
- Consultations: patient communication and video.
- Clinical workspace: consent-aware chart, history, observations, encounter, diagnosis, treatment and amendments.
- Specialist referrals: incoming/outgoing referral coordination and feedback.
- Laboratory referrals: test ordering and results.
- Imaging: radiology ordering and released reports.
- Prescriptions: create, sign and route e-prescriptions.
- Inpatients: admissions, bed, rounds, tasks and discharge planning.
- Earnings and payouts.
- Professional credentials and facility affiliations.
- Support, notifications and security.

## Nurse / midwife / theatre nurse

Pages:

- Nursing dashboard and assigned queue.
- Triage: chief complaint, urgency and observations.
- Vitals and nursing assessment.
- Nursing notes.
- Medication administration.
- Ward tasks and handover.
- Admissions and ward occupancy.
- Maternity episodes.
- Theatre cases and recovery tasks.
- Shift schedule and leave.
- Security and notifications.

## Receptionist / records officer

Pages:

- Front-desk dashboard.
- Patient search and registration.
- Walk-in registration.
- Appointment calendar and provider schedules.
- Check-in and queue.
- Department routing.
- Patient demographic correction.
- Records requests and privacy routing.

Reception permissions do not permit clinical diagnosis, signed-note editing, result validation or payment approval.

## Cashier / accountant

Pages:

- Billing dashboard.
- Pending invoices.
- Itemized invoice creation.
- Partial/full payment collection.
- PDF invoice and receipt.
- Email invoice.
- Cashier shift and variance.
- Refund and adjustment queue according to role.
- Reconciliation and finance reports.

## Pharmacist / storekeeper

Pages:

- Pharmacy dashboard.
- Prescription queue.
- Clinical dispensing and partial fills.
- Patient counselling notes.
- Medication catalogue.
- Customer orders.
- Inventory, batches and expiry.
- Suppliers.
- Purchase orders and stock receiving.
- Stock movements and low-stock reports.

Only pharmacist roles can clinically dispense; storekeepers manage stock and procurement.

## Laboratory professional

Pages:

- Laboratory dashboard.
- Incoming orders.
- Test catalogue and panels.
- Specimen collection/custody.
- Barcode tracking.
- Result entry.
- Validation and release.
- Critical-result acknowledgement.
- Laboratory inventory and reports.

## Radiologist / radiographer

Pages:

- Imaging worklist.
- Scheduling and performance.
- Study status.
- Findings and impression.
- Critical-result flag.
- Report verification and release.

## Emergency team

Pages:

- Emergency board.
- Triage and severity.
- Resuscitation/escalation state.
- Ambulance dispatch and crew assignment.
- Pickup, destination and transfer.
- Emergency admissions and tasks.

## Theatre team

Pages:

- Theatre schedule.
- Surgical case.
- Team assignment.
- Consent and safety checklist.
- Pre-operative readiness.
- Intra-operative state.
- Recovery and post-operative notes.

## Facility/organization management

Pages:

- Operations dashboard.
- Branch/facility identity and licence.
- Departments.
- Staff invitations and directory.
- Role/permission assignments.
- Shift and leave management.
- Queue and service performance.
- Wards, beds and admissions.
- Inventory and procurement.
- Billing oversight and financial controls.
- Emergency, theatre and maternity operations.
- Quality incidents and privacy requests.
- Notifications, subscriptions, support and enterprise analytics.

## Insurance organization / reviewer

Pages:

- Coverage dashboard.
- Policies and member eligibility.
- Claim submission/review queue.
- Approval/rejection.
- Workforce, permissions and schedule.
- Notifications, subscription, support and organization profile.

## Platform administrator

Pages:

- Command centre.
- Users, account states and roles.

- Credential and licence review.
- Hospital operations.
- Enterprise analytics.
- Emergency and theatre oversight.
- Appointment monitoring.
- Quality incidents.
- Finance, refunds and adjustments.
- Subscriptions.

- Support operations.
- Consent, privacy and break-glass review.
